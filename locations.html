Which location has the 24/7 drive-thru kiosk?

Only Meridian Road at 1800 S. Meridian Rd. #105. Clothesline prints that there is one kiosk. Text “Kiosk” to 208-428-6408 to open it.

Why does Google say Green Leaf Cleaners?

The live site: the Linder Road location may still appear publicly on Google as Green Leaf Cleaners. It is the Linder Road store at 6700 N. Linder Rd. Ste 144 in Meridian. “Our Linder Road location in north Meridian is currently signed and listed as Green Leaf Cleaners.”

Do hours conflict on the live site?

Lobby hours match across the locations and contact pages. Contact adds Sunday closed (the locations page does not list Sunday). Linder’s Monday–Thursday close is 5:30pm; Boise and Meridian Road close at 5pm. Those differences are printed, not guessed.
