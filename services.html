Garment care, not a menu maze.

Family-owned dry cleaning and garment care with 46 years serving Ada County and Canyon County. Services printed on the live site: dry cleaning, laundry, alterations, pickup and delivery, wedding gown care, leather and suede, bedding, drapes and curtains, area rugs, pillows, patio cushions, commercial work, and textile restoration.

Bridal

Wedding gown cleaning & preservation

Clothesline is connected with the Association of Wedding Gown Specialists, offers MuseumCare and Foster-Stephens preservation options, and prints that The Knot and WeddingWire voted them the top bridal care destination in Idaho in 2026.

Services as printed: pre-wedding inspection, pressing and finishing, alterations and touch-ups, beading and trim care, stain inspection, certified preservation.

Starting prices from the live price list (April 29, 2026): press only from $109.99; cleaned & pressed no train from $239.99; with train from $299.99; cleaned & preserved no train from $359.98; with train from $419.98; preserved only (Foster Stephens) from $119.99. Final price varies with train, fabric, construction, staining, trim, and beadwork.

Executive shirts and everyday dry cleaning

Professional shirt and blouse cleaning, finishing, and pressing. Laundry machine-pressed shirts/blouses print at $4.25–$6.49; hand finished $9.99–$16.99. Two-piece suits $24–$38. Regular dresses $18.99–$39.99. See the price list for the rest as printed April 29, 2026.

Alterations and repairs

Hems, zipper repair and replacement, linings, patches and trims, mending, snaps/clasps/buttons, and private fittings. If the garment also needs cleaning, Clothesline prefers to clean first, then alter. If you only want alteration work, send it clean and dry.

Meridian Road has an on-site expert seamstress Monday through Friday. Route pickup works if you pin a note. Pant hems print at $20–$49 depending on construction.

Household, flatwork, pillows, rugs

Comforters, duvets, blankets, quilts, mattress pads, sleeping bags, pet bedding, drapes, curtains, premium bedding, sheets, table linens. Patio cushions too. Household copy prints the Treasure Valley’s only flatiron. Commercial copy: flatiron can press items up to 10 feet wide and unlimited length.

Pillow cleaning: down, feather-filled, memory foam, and synthetic. The pillow page heading prints “7 Limp Pillows Remade Into 4 Plump Pillows.” Bed pillow, down-filled: $20–$59 on the April 2026 list.

Area rugs: inspection, dust removal, spot and odor treatment, wash/rinse/extraction, controlled drying. Wool, oriental and Persian-style, synthetic, handmade, machine-loomed, leather and specialty rugs after review. Non-machine-washable area rugs: $4.89 per sq. ft.

Leather, suede, and fur

Leather jackets (cleaning, conditioning, odor, scuff review, color touch-up, re-dye), suede, handbags and purses, boots and UGG®, fur cleaning and glazing, leather repairs, re-dye and edge paint, designer pieces, bags and luggage. Not every stain or water mark can be removed — the team reviews each item. Many specialty pieces can start on route pickup with a note.

Commercial

Uniforms (including public safety), medical uniforms with biohazard cleaning when needed, service businesses, offices, events and linen needs, local businesses in Boise, Meridian, Ada County, Canyon County, and nearby Treasure Valley communities. Recurring pickup can be set up for businesses.

Textile restoration is listed on the live services hub as support for textiles, garments, and household fabric items. Details beyond that heading were not copied into this brochure.
