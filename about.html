Certifications as printed

Since 2006, Clothesline has earned the Seal of Approval for Quality Garment Cleaning and Customer Service from the Clothing Care Council. To date, approximately 140 companies around the world have attained this award, which requires passing separate stain removal and cleaning performance tests, among other criteria. Clothesline Cleaners is the ONLY drycleaner in Idaho to have ever earned the award.

Clothesline Cleaners is Idaho’s first and only Certified Garment Care Professional (CGCP), Certified Environmental Drycleaner (CED) and Association of Wedding Gown Specialists. In addition, they are a Certified Professional Drycleaner (CPD) and Certified Professional Wetcleaner (CPW).

The team continues training through industry education, including the Drycleaning and Laundry Institute (DLI). The about page also lists connections with the Idaho Dry Cleaners Association, the Boise Metro Chamber of Commerce, and the Meridian Chamber of Commerce.

In 2026 The Knot and WeddingWire voted them as the top bridal care destination in Idaho. A blog post dated April 9, 2026 prints recognition in the Dry Cleaner category as Idaho’s Best Silver and Best of Treasure Valley for 2026.

They print that they conduct secret shopper audits to make sure staff is friendly, helpful, proficient and professional, and that they invest in wages, benefits, and support so the team can take better care of customers.

K4 since 2016

Clothesline has high standards for safety and environmental responsibility. Since 2016 they have used K4, a premium dry cleaning solvent designed to be effective, gentle, and environmentally responsible. Printed claims: non-toxic and safe with no known health risks to you; dermatologically tested and rated “very good,” including for sensitive skin; odorless; biodegradable; low VOCs; strong stain removal on oil-based and water-soluble stains; gentle on fabrics; extended garment life; Idaho’s first and only ever Certified Environmental Drycleaner.
