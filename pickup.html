Free route pickup and delivery.

Clothesline prints this as free route pickup and delivery for eligible dry cleaning and laundry orders in Boise, Meridian, and nearby Treasure Valley route areas. Scheduling happens in their SMRT customer portal — we link out; we do not fake a login.

How it works

1

Schedule

Request route service online for your home or office and create your Clothesline Cleaners account.

2

Place your order

Use your route bag or door hook location so your driver can collect garments securely on pickup day.

3

Professional care

Dry cleaning, laundry, and eligible household items are cleaned, finished, and checked by the team.

4

Delivery back

Clean items return to your home or office on your regular route schedule.

What you can send

The live pickup page says route pickup works well for everyday dry cleaning, laundry, wash and fold, eligible household items, and many repair or alteration requests when clear notes are included.

  • Dry cleaning — professional and casual garments
  • Laundry and shirts
  • Wash and fold
  • Eligible bedding and household textiles
  • Alterations and repairs with pinned notes

Sending alterations by route? Pin a note to the garment or include written instructions in your route bag. The team can review the request and contact you if measurements, approval, or more detail is needed.

Coverage as printed

Routes are organized around Boise and Meridian. If you are near the edge of the route area, including parts of Ada County or Canyon County, create an account or review the route service guide to confirm your address. Offices can be set up when the location is within an available route area.

The services hub also prints: “Our free pickup and delivery services covers all Ada and Canyon county and some areas beyond that.”

Timing depends on your scheduled route days and the type of items. The live site points to a PDF route service guide for details — we do not invent turnaround times.

Pickup questions

Is pickup and delivery really free?

Yes. Clothesline Cleaners offers free route pickup and delivery for eligible dry cleaning and laundry orders in their Boise and Meridian route areas.

Can you pick up from my office?

Yes, when the address is within an available route area.

What happens after I create an account?

After the account is created in the SMRT portal, you can request pickup, review route details, and follow the instructions for where to place garments.

Need a store instead?

Visit any of the three counters, or use the Meridian Road 24/7 kiosk for after-hours drop-off and pickup that fits the window.
