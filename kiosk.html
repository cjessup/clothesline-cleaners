Live copy: most ready orders can use the kiosk. Long or bulky items need inside pickup. If you have trouble, the support number is listed on the kiosk under Support — use that number only if you are having a kiosk problem.

Best uses as printed: standard dry cleaning, shirts and blouses, after-hours drop-off, ready-order pickup, commute-friendly I-84 stop. The kiosk is connected to the real Meridian Road store and garment care team, not an unattended third-party service.

Kiosk questions

Is there more than one kiosk?

No. Clothesline Cleaners has one 24/7 kiosk, and it is at the Meridian Road location.

Can I pick up every item at the kiosk?

No. Finished items longer than 60 inches or bulky items must be picked up inside because they do not fit through the outbound glass window.

Boise drop box vs this kiosk?

The Boise Orchard store is printed as having a 24-hour drop box. That is not the same as the Meridian Road kiosk.

Need bulky pickup instead?

Use Meridian Road during lobby hours, or schedule route pickup for eligible items.
